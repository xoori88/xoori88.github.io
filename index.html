yourdomain.com/about

The communicator who learned to speak search.

I studied Mass Communication because I was fascinated by how messages find their audience. SEO turned out to be the modern version of that exact problem: billions of searches happen every day, and most businesses are invisible for the ones that matter to them. I made it my job to fix that.

My transition into SEO wasn't a career pivot so much as a natural evolution — I went from writing for audiences to engineering how audiences find the writing. I completed the Google SEO Specialization, then immediately put it to work: building websites from scratch, running keyword research, restructuring site architecture, fixing technical issues, and optimizing Google Business Profiles for local visibility.

What drives me is solving search problems. A page that should rank but doesn't is a puzzle — and the answer usually lives somewhere between crawlability, content, intent, and authority. I enjoy tracing that path, whether it's in Google Search Console data, a competitor's backlink profile, or an app store keyword field.

I work best in collaborative teams — with developers, writers, designers, and stakeholders — because SEO touches everything, and I can translate between "search engine" and "human." And because search never stops changing (hello, AI Overviews), I never stop learning: AEO and GEO are already part of my toolkit, not buzzwords on my radar.
